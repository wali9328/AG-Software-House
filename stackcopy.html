About StackCopy

StackCopy is a browser extension designed for developers who frequently work with code snippets, frameworks, configurations, and technical documentation.

It provides a convenient way to copy useful code, maintain a local clipboard history, and keep frequently used development content organized while working across different websites and projects.

⚡ One-Click Copy Quickly copy code and useful content without unnecessary formatting.
📋 Clipboard History Keep your recently copied development content available through the local history dashboard.
🔄 Framework Detection Automatically recognize common framework syntax and development structures.
🛡 Local Data Designed to keep your clipboard history locally available for your development workflow.
🆕 Lifetime Updates Premium users receive lifetime updates and ongoing improvements.
💻 Developer Focused Built specifically to make everyday developer workflows faster and more convenient.
