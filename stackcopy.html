About StackCopy

Stop switching browser tabs 50 times just to copy and paste data entry points one by one.

The standard browser clipboard is a productivity trap. The moment you highlight and copy a new sentence, your previous copy vanishes into thin air. It breaks your concentration, wastes hours of manual labor, and makes online research a painful chore.

Generic "free" clipboard managers claim to solve this—but at what cost? Most free tools quietly track your keystrokes, log your browsing history, and sell your private data to advertising corporations. Every time you copy a password, a client's email, or private financial records, you are exposed.

StackCopy changes everything.

StackCopy is a lightning-fast, distraction-free multi-clipboard built directly for your browser. It lets you stack dozens of text fragments and raw image pixels into one organized, floating dashboard using simple shortcuts.

⚡ Lightning Fast Capture multiple pieces of content without constantly switching between tabs and applications.
📋 Multi-Clipboard Stack dozens of copied text fragments instead of losing your previous clipboard item.
🖼 Text & Images Store text fragments and raw image pixels together in one organized dashboard.
🎯 Distraction-Free Stay focused on your research and data-entry workflow without repeatedly jumping between tabs.
🔒 Privacy Focused StackCopy is designed around a privacy-first workflow, keeping your copied information under your control.
⌨️ Simple Shortcuts Quickly capture and organize content using convenient keyboard shortcuts.
